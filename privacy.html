## Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** August 21, 2026

**PrayerPrompts** (“the App”) is a prayer companion application provided for worship and personal devotion. This policy describes how the App handles information.

### Who we are

- App name: PrayerPrompts
- Contact: prayerprompts@newlife.church
- Website: https://www.prayerprompts.com

### Summary

- We do **not** require an account or login.
- We do **not** sell your personal information.
- We do **not** use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs in the App as currently distributed.
- Preferences you choose in the App are stored **on your device**.
- Optional **local notifications** (phrase-transition reminders) are controlled by you and use Apple’s on-device notification system.

### Information we do not collect

The App does **not** collect:

- Name, email, or account credentials (there is no sign-up)
- Location history for tracking
- Contacts, photos, or microphone recordings for cloud upload
- Advertising identifiers for cross-app tracking

### Information stored on your device

The App may store settings locally on your iPhone or iPad, such as:

- Appearance (light / dark / system)
- Preferred spoken voice and related playback preferences
- Guided Prayers setup choices (template, duration, voice style)
- Whether phrase-transition notifications are enabled

This data stays on your device unless you back up your device through Apple services you control (for example iCloud Backup). We do not operate a PrayerPrompts user database for these preferences.

### Notifications

If you enable phrase-transition notifications and grant permission, the App may schedule **local** notifications on your device. These are not used to send marketing messages from a remote server. You can turn them off in the App (About → Settings) or in iOS Settings → Notifications.

### Audio and content

Guided prompts and Pray Scripture audio play on your device. Content is bundled with the App or generated on-device (for example system speech). We do not upload your prayer session history to our servers.

### Children

The App is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. There is no account creation.

### Third parties

As currently distributed, the App does not embed third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. Distribution through the Apple App Store is subject to Apple’s privacy practices for App Store and device services.

### Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App’s behavior changes (for example if analytics are added later). The “Last updated” date will change. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.

### Contact

Questions about privacy: **prayerprompts@newlife.church**
